Tuna Onigiri

Description

Tuna onigiri is a easy recipe to make and is a excellent snack or something quick to eat and it has many different combinations and things you can add into it but today I will tell you how to make a basic version of this delicious food.

Ingredients

  • Short grain rice
  • Canned tuna
  • Japanese mayo
  • Soy sauce
  • Seaweed
  • Sriracha sauce if you want a little kick to it

Steps

  1. First step is to drain the tuna from the can and then add it to a bowl along with the Japanese mayo and soy sauce and give it a nice stir
  2. Second is to wet your hands with water and put a scoop of rice into the palm of your hand and mold it a little bit into a base
  3. After you make a little base of rice in your palm put about a spoon scoop of the tuna that we mixed on top of the rice
  4. Now add another scoop of rice on top of everything and use both hand to mold it together, do a clock wise motion to help mold a triangle shape
  5. After shaping it press on all 3 sides to shape the triangle better and then finally wrap it with seaweed or cut it into rectangle shapes to wrap at the bottom of the onigiri

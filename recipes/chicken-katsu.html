Chicken Katsu

Description

Chicken katsu is a way of fried chicken served along rice with a drizzle of sauce on top of it

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Skinless boneless chicken
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • All purpose flour
  • Panko crumbs
  • Egg
  • Oil
  • Tonkatsu sauce

Steps

  1. Flatten your chicken
  2. season the chicken with salt and pepper on both sides
  3. Then put the chicken into flour and make sure it is evenly coated on both sides
  4. Do the previous step again but into a beaten egg and then finally into the panko crumbs
  5. Heat a pot with oil enough to cover the chicken halfway
  6. while the pot is heating cook rice in a rice cooker
  7. Once the oil is ready place the chicken into it and cook for about 5 minutes then flip or fry until golden brown
  8. When finish frying lay the chicken on a wire rack and let rest for about 5 minutes so the excess oil can drip out
  9. Finally on a plate or bowl scoop rice onto it and cut the chicken into even pieces and place on top of rice and finish it with a drizzle of tonkatsu sauce

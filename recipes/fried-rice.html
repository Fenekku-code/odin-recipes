Fried Rice

Description

Fried rice is a delicious dish to make with left over refrigerated cooked rice, same as onigiri fried rice can have different ingredients added to it such as chicken or sausage and different types of veggies.

As before I will keep these recipes basic but you can add and make changes to it.

Ingredients

  • Left over refrigerated rice
  • 2 Eggs
  • Soy sauce
  • Salt
  • Oil
  • Green onion

Steps

  1. In a pan add oil and heat it up
  2. crack open the eggs and add it into the pan and mix them as they cook, you want to cook them to the point where it cooks most of the way and still a little liquidly
  3. Throw in the rice and mix the egg and rice together, make sure to break the clumps of rice as you move them around the pan
  4. Once the rice is all separated from each other add in the soy sauce and salt
  5. Give it a good mix then take it from the pan onto a plate or into a bowl
  6. Slice the green onion into small pieces and use it to garnish the fried rice
